# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders and operators at early-stage B2B companies (and their investor partners) who need an operating system for growth — not another generic dashboard. They arrive after selecting their company and want clarity on benchmarks, track health, and next actions.

## Product Purpose

Fuel by York IE turns company context, track details, and private benchmarks into an Overview scorecard, intelligence timeline, and partner-ready recommendations. Success is a completed profile that unlocks analytics — not a blank workspace.

## Brand Personality

Precise, operator-grade, quietly confident. Voice is direct and domain-fluent (ARR, cohort, GTM), never hypey. Three words: **sharp · grounded · enabling**.

## Anti-references

Generic purple SaaS dashboards, cream-and-serif “AI startup” marketing shells, empty lorem onboarding, decorative glassmorphism, and celebration-first empty states that hide the next required action.

## Design Principles

1. **Activation before analytics** — incomplete profiles see a clear unlock path; scores stay locked until details exist.
2. **One job per surface** — onboarding collects identity; the workspace collects operating depth.
3. **Familiar product affordances** — select, form, banner, sidebar; no invented controls.
4. **York IE as partner, not chrome** — partner nudges and credits appear only when the user can act on them.
5. **Real company language** — Patriot Pay, Ramp, Seed, FinTech — never placeholder copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on dark onboarding and light app shell. Visible focus rings on forms and nav. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for onboarding motion panels.
