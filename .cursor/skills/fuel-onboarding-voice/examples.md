## What not to write

```
❌ Hi — I'm Fuel, your smart operating advisor. The kind that doesn't take equity or advisory fees — just guidance built from your numbers, your market, and how you run the company. (too long; save for pricing)
❌ It looks like you might be signed in as...
❌ We're excited to help you unlock growth!
❌ Please confirm if this is correct for your organization.
```
